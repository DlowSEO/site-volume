Site Volume is installed

Set a volume for each website once. It remembers.

  1. Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to Site Volume.

  2. Play something and click the icon

    Drag the slider, use the presets, or scroll over the popup. Up to 200% with a built-in limiter so boosted sound doesn't crackle.

  3. Tick Remember

    That site gets the same volume every time you visit. Your browser asks once for access to that one site, which is how Site Volume avoids needing access to every site you visit.

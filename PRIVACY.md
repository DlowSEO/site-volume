# Site Volume privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

**Site Volume does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or advertising.**

## What is stored, and where

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The volume, mute and option settings for each website you tick **Remember** on, stored by website name (for example `youtube.com`) | Your browser's extension storage (`storage.sync`). If you're signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser syncs it between your own computers. It never reaches us. | So the site gets the same volume next time |
| Your settings (maximum boost, limiter) | Same as above | To apply your preferences |
| The level you set on the current tab | Your browser's session storage, cleared when the browser closes | To keep the level while you stay on that site |
| A count of how many times you've opened the popup and when you first used it | Your browser's local extension storage, on this computer only | To ask once, after a few days of use, whether you'd like to leave a rating |

Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it. Unticking **Remember** for a site deletes that site's entry straight away.

## Audio

Site Volume changes audio inside your browser as it plays. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

**Force boost** (optional, Chrome and Edge only) uses the browser's tab capture to process a tab's sound when the page itself can't be adjusted. The captured sound goes straight back to your speakers and is never recorded or transmitted.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves the settings described above.
- **activeTab** and **scripting**: when you click the icon or use a shortcut, Site Volume can adjust sound on that one tab.
- **offscreen** (Chrome and Edge): plays the boosted sound back when Force boost is on.
- **Access to a site** (optional): requested one site at a time when you tick **Remember**, so the volume applies automatically when you visit. You can optionally allow all sites to control every tab from the popup. Site Volume never reads or changes page content beyond the audio and video elements.
- **tabCapture** (optional, Chrome and Edge): requested only when you switch on Force boost.
